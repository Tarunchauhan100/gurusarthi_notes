Privacy Policy — Guru Saathi Notes

Last Updated: September 18, 2026

Guru Saathi Notes ("we", "our", or "the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information and permissions are handled when you use the Guru Saathi Notes application.

1. Information We Collect

Guru Saathi Notes is designed as a local notes and file-management application.

We do not require users to create an account or sign in to use the application.

We do not directly collect or maintain personal information such as:

Name
Email address
Phone number
Password
Contacts
Account information
Location information
Personal identification information

The content you create or add to the application is intended to remain on your device.

2. Files and Notes

Guru Saathi Notes allows you to create and manage folders and store content such as:

Notes
Images
Videos
PDFs and documents
Other supported files
Recordings

Files and content that you add to the application are stored locally on your device for the application's functionality.

We do not upload your personal files or notes to our servers.

3. App and Folder Lock

Guru Saathi Notes may provide security features such as:

Application lock
Folder lock
Protected folders
Access protection for private content

These features are intended to help you protect content stored within the application.

We do not receive your lock credentials or protected content on our servers.

Important: Users should keep their device secure and remember their chosen lock credentials. Loss of access caused by forgotten credentials may not be recoverable.

4. Camera Permission

Guru Saathi Notes may request Camera permission when you choose to use camera-related functionality.

Camera access may be used to:

Capture photos
Capture content for notes
Scan or capture documents where supported

The camera is accessed only when required for the selected feature.

We do not use the camera to continuously monitor you or collect photographs in the background.

5. Microphone and Recording

If Guru Saathi Notes provides audio or video recording functionality, the application may request Microphone permission when you choose to record.

Recorded content is stored locally on your device for use within the application.

We do not upload your recordings to our servers.

6. Photos, Videos and File Access

The application may request appropriate Android permissions when you choose to select, create, view, or manage photos, videos, PDFs, documents, or other supported files.

These permissions are used only to provide the corresponding functionality requested by you.

Guru Saathi Notes does not intentionally collect or transmit your personal files to us.

7. No Cloud Backup

Guru Saathi Notes does not provide a cloud backup service and does not automatically upload your notes, folders, images, videos, PDFs, recordings, or other personal content to our servers.

If you uninstall the application, clear its app data, or lose your device, locally stored application data may be lost.

Users are responsible for maintaining their own copies of important files where necessary.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

We do not share your notes, folders, images, videos, PDFs, recordings, or other locally stored personal content with third parties.

9. Third-Party Services

Guru Saathi Notes does not require a user account or cloud storage service for its core functionality.

If third-party services are introduced in future versions, this Privacy Policy may be updated accordingly.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to design the application so that your locally stored content remains under your control.

However, no electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We recommend keeping your Android device protected with a secure device lock and maintaining copies of important information when necessary.

11. Children's Privacy

The application does not require users to provide personal information or create an online account to use its core features.

12. Your Control
